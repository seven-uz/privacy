Privacy Policy

Oxirgi yangilanish / Last updated: 2026-07-04

Ilova / App: Hifz — Qur'on yodlash (com.sevenuz.hifz)

O'zbekcha

Hifz ilovasi foydalanuvchi maxfiyligini to'liq hurmat qiladi.

Ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi. Hifz hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumotni yig'maydi, saqlamaydi va uchinchi tomonlarga uzatmaydi. Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish yoki akkaunt talab qilinmaydi.

Ma'lumot qayerda saqlanadi. Siz kiritgan yodlash ma'lumotlari (sura, oyat, sahifa, juz', izohlar, statistika, sozlamalar) faqat qurilmangizning ichki xotirasida (lokal Hive ma'lumotlar bazasi) saqlanadi. Bu ma'lumot serverlarga yuborilmaydi.

Internet. Ilova ishlashi uchun internet talab qilinmaydi.

Ruxsatlar. Ilova faqat kunlik takror eslatmalarini ko'rsatish uchun bildirishnoma ruxsatidan foydalanadi (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATE). Bu ruxsatlar hech qanday ma'lumot yig'ish uchun ishlatilmaydi.

Zaxira. Zaxira/tiklash funksiyasi ma'lumotni siz tanlagan faylga eksport qiladi; bu fayl to'liq sizning nazoratingizda bo'ladi.

Bolalar. Ilova har qanday yoshdagi foydalanuvchilar uchun xavfsiz, chunki hech qanday ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi.

Aloqa: uzseven.uz@gmail.com

English

The Hifz app fully respects user privacy.

No data collection. Hifz does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any third party. No sign-up or account is required.

Where data is stored. The memorization data you enter (surah, ayah, page, juz, notes, statistics, settings) is stored only in your device's internal storage (a local Hive database). This data is never sent to any server.

Internet. No internet connection is required for the app to work.

Permissions. The app uses notification permissions only to show daily review reminders (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATE). These are not used to collect any data.

Backup. The backup/restore feature exports data to a file you choose; that file remains entirely under your control.

Children. The app is safe for users of all ages, as no data is collected.
