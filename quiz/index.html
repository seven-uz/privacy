English

Queezy is a gamified quiz / trivia app. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data — email address, username and (hashed) password when you create an account or sign in with a social provider (Google / Facebook).
  • Profile data — display name, avatar and the country/region you choose.
  • Gameplay data — quizzes you create or play, scores, points, badges and leaderboard standings.
  • Microphone / audio — for the Voice Note question type only, the app records short audio clips when you explicitly tap record. Audio is used to create and answer voice questions and is uploaded only as part of a quiz you submit. We do not listen in the background.

How we use it

To provide the core features: authentication, creating and playing quizzes, scoring, leaderboards and badges. To show your profile and ranking to other players.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers required to run the app (authentication, hosting).

Your choices

You can decline the microphone permission; only the Voice Note feature is affected. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.

Children

Queezy is not directed at children under 13.

Queezy — gamifikatsiyalangan viktorina ilovasi. Ushbu siyosat qanday ma'lumot yig'ilishini va nima uchun ishlatilishini tushuntiradi.

Yig'iladigan ma'lumotlar

  • Akkaunt — email, foydalanuvchi nomi va (shifrlangan) parol; yoki ijtimoiy tarmoq (Google / Facebook) orqali kirish ma'lumotlari.
  • Profil — ism, avatar va tanlagan davlat/mintaqangiz.
  • O'yin — yaratgan yoki o'ynagan viktorinalar, ochkolar, ballar, nishonlar va reytingdagi o'rin.
  • Mikrofon / audio — faqat Voice Note (ovozli javob) savol turi uchun. Ilova ovozni faqat siz "yozish" tugmasini bosganingizda yozib oladi. Audio faqat siz yuborgan viktorina tarkibida yuklanadi; fonda tinglanmaydi.

Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish

Asosiy funksiyalar uchun: kirish/ro'yxatdan o'tish, viktorina yaratish va o'ynash, ball hisoblash, reyting va nishonlar. Profilingiz va o'rningizni boshqa o'yinchilarga ko'rsatish uchun.

Sizning tanlovingiz
