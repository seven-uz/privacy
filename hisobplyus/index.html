Privacy Policy for Hisob+

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Hisob+ Calculator app.

1. Data Collection

We collect basic information (Telegram Name and User ID) only when you explicitly log in using our Telegram Bot for the purpose of cloud synchronization of your calculator history.

2. Data Security

All data is transmitted securely over HTTPS and stored securely. We do not share your data with any third parties.

3. Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your account and all associated calculation history at any time by contacting us.

4. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@sevenuz.uz.
