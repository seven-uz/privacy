O'zbekcha

Mochi — bu oddiy arcade o'yini. Biz foydalanuvchining shaxsiy ma'lumotlarini yig'maymiz, saqlamaymiz va uchinchi tomonlarga uzatmaymiz.

  • Ma'lumot yig'ish: Ilova hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot (ism, email, joylashuv, kontaktlar va h.k.) yig'maydi.
  • Mahalliy saqlash: Faqat sizning eng yuqori balingiz (rekord) qurilmangizning o'zida saqlanadi. Bu ma'lumot internetga yuborilmaydi.
  • Internet: Ilova ishlash uchun internetga ulanishni talab qilmaydi va hech qanday serverga ma'lumot yubormaydi.
  • Reklama va tahlil: Ilovada uchinchi tomon reklamalari yoki analitika xizmatlari yo'q.
  • Bolalar: Ilova barcha yoshdagilar uchun xavfsiz va hech qanday ma'lumot yig'maydi.

Savollar bo'lsa: uzseven.uz@gmail.com

English

Mochi is a simple arcade game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • Data collection: The app collects no personal information (name, email, location, contacts, etc.).
  • Local storage: Only your high score is stored locally on your device. It is never transmitted over the internet.
  • Internet: The app does not require an internet connection and sends no data to any server.
  • Ads & analytics: The app contains no third-party ads or analytics services.
  • Children: The app is safe for all ages and collects no data.

Questions: uzseven.uz@gmail.com
