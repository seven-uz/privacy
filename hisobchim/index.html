O'zbekcha

1. Umumiy ma'lumot

Hisobchim (keyingi o'rinlarda — "ilova") shaxsiy moliyani boshqarish uchun mobil ilova. Ilova foydalanuvchining daromad va xarajatlarini hisobga olish imkonini beradi. Mazkur siyosat ilova qanday ma'lumot to'plashi va ulardan qanday foydalanishini tushuntiradi.

2. To'planadigan ma'lumotlar

Ilova shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni serverga yubormaydi va to'plamaydi. Quyidagilar faqat sizning qurilmangizda lokal saqlanadi:

  • Hisoblar (nomi, balans, valyuta)
  • Toifalar (nomi, ikonka, rang)
  • Tranzaksiyalar (summa, sana, izoh, toifa va hisob bog'lanmasi)
  • Byudjetlar (toifa bo'yicha oylik limit)
  • Takroriy tranzaksiyalar (shablonlar)
  • Sozlamalar (mavzu, asosiy valyuta)
  • PIN kod (SHA-256 algoritmi bilan hashlangan ko'rinishda)

3. Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Barcha ma'lumotlar qurilmangizdagi lokal SQLite ma'lumotlar bazasi va SharedPreferences xotirasida saqlanadi. Hech qanday cloud yoki tashqi server foydalanilmaydi.

4. Tarmoq va tashqi xizmatlar

Ilova internet ulanishini talab qilmaydi. Faqat quyidagi holatlarda tizim funksiyalari ishga tushadi:

  • Ulashish (Share) — eksport faylini boshqa ilovalarga uzatish uchun Android tizim oynasidan foydalanadi.
  • Bildirishnomalar — eslatma uchun lokal notification (Google serveri ishlatilmaydi).

English

1. Overview

Hisobchim ("the app") is a personal finance management mobile application. It lets users record income and expenses. This policy explains what data the app processes and how.

2. Data we process

The app does not transmit or collect any personal data on a server. The following items are stored locally on your device only:

  • Accounts (name, balance, currency)
  • Categories (name, icon, color)
  • Transactions (amount, date, note, category and account link)
  • Budgets (monthly limit per category)
  • Recurring transactions (templates)
  • Settings (theme, default currency)
  • PIN code (stored as a SHA-256 hash, never as plain text)

3. Where data is stored

All data is stored in a local SQLite database and SharedPreferences on your device. The app uses no cloud or remote storage.

4. Network and external services

The app does not require an internet connection. The following operating-system features may be invoked on demand:

  • Share sheet — for exporting data to other apps via Android's system share dialog.
  • Local notifications — for the daily reminder (no Google/push servers involved).

6. Export and deletion

You can export your data at any time as JSON or CSV. To delete all data, simply uninstall the app — all records are removed from device storage.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

8. Third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. It relies only on open-source libraries (Flutter, SQLite, etc.).
