Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 28-iyul

Ilova: Obunalarim (com.sevenuz.obunanazorat)

O'zbekcha

1. Umumiy ma'lumot

Obunalarim (keyingi o'rinlarda — "ilova") obunalar va takroriy to'lovlarni kuzatish uchun mobil ilova. Ilova foydalanuvchiga qaysi xizmatlarga obuna bo'lganini, to'lov sanalarini va oylik/yillik xarajatni ko'rish imkonini beradi. Mazkur siyosat ilova qanday ma'lumot bilan ishlashini tushuntiradi.

2. To'planadigan ma'lumotlar

Ilova shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni serverga yubormaydi va to'plamaydi. Quyidagilar faqat sizning qurilmangizda lokal saqlanadi:

  • Obunalar (nomi, summa, valyuta, davri)
  • To'lov sanalari va tarixi
  • Toifalar va belgilar
  • Eslatma sozlamalari
  • Ilova sozlamalari (mavzu, asosiy valyuta)

Ro'yxatdan o'tish, hisob yaratish yoki tizimga kirish talab qilinmaydi.

3. Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Barcha ma'lumotlar qurilmangizdagi lokal SQLite ma'lumotlar bazasi va SharedPreferences xotirasida saqlanadi. Hech qanday cloud yoki tashqi server foydalanilmaydi.

4. Tarmoq va tashqi xizmatlar

  • Valyuta kurslari — ilova valyuta kurslarini O'zbekiston Markaziy banki ochiq API'sidan (cbu.uz) oladi. Faqat ochiq kurs ma'lumotlari o'qiladi; hech qanday shaxsiy, moliyaviy yoki qurilma ma'lumoti yuborilmaydi. Bu ixtiyoriy — internetsiz ilova saqlangan kurslar bilan ishlaydi.
  • Bildirishnomalar — to'lov eslatmalari uchun lokal notification (Google yoki push serverlari ishlatilmaydi).

Ilovada reklama, analitika va uchinchi tomon kuzatuv SDK'lari yo'q.

6. Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Ma'lumotlarni butunlay o'chirish uchun ilovani o'chirib tashlash kifoya — barcha yozuvlar qurilma xotirasidan yo'qoladi. Ilova ichida ham har bir obunani alohida o'chirish mumkin.

7. Bolalar maxfiyligi

Ilova 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalardan biron-bir ma'lumot to'plamaydi va ular uchun mo'ljallanmagan.

8. Moliyaviy xizmatlar emas

Ilova to'lovlarni amalga oshirmaydi, pul o'tkazmaydi, kredit bermaydi va bank xizmati ko'rsatmaydi. U faqat foydalanuvchi o'zi kiritgan ma'lumotlarni eslab turadigan shaxsiy hisob daftarchasidir.

English

1. Overview

Obunalarim ("the app") is a mobile application for tracking subscriptions and recurring payments. It lets users see which services they are subscribed to, when payments are due, and what they spend monthly or yearly. This policy explains what data the app processes and how.

2. Data we process

The app does not transmit or collect any personal data on a server. The following items are stored locally on your device only:

  • Subscriptions (name, amount, currency, billing period)
  • Payment dates and history
  • Categories and labels
  • Reminder settings
  • App settings (theme, default currency)

No registration, account or sign-in is required.

3. Where data is stored

All data is stored in a local SQLite database and SharedPreferences on your device. The app uses no cloud or remote storage.

4. Network and external services

  • Currency exchange rates — the app reads exchange rates from the public API of the Central Bank of Uzbekistan (cbu.uz). Only public rate data is retrieved; no personal, financial or device information is ever sent. This is optional — the app works offline using stored rates.
  • Local notifications — for payment reminders (no Google or push servers involved).

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.

6. Deletion

To delete all data, simply uninstall the app — all records are removed from device storage. Individual subscriptions can also be deleted inside the app at any time.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 and is not directed at them.

8. Not a financial service

The app does not process payments, transfer money, offer credit, or provide any banking service. It is a personal record-keeping tool for data the user enters themselves.
