TwinPane — file manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-17

TwinPane ("the app") is a file manager for Android. Your privacy matters, and the app is designed to keep everything on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. TwinPane does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no network calls of any kind in this version.

Storage access

To let you browse and manage your files, the app requests access to your device storage:

  • On Android 11+ it uses the All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) permission, which is necessary for a file manager to view and operate on files anywhere on the device.
  • On Android 10 and below it uses the standard read/write storage permission.

This access is used only on your device, in direct response to your actions (opening a folder, copying, moving, deleting, extracting an archive, etc.). File contents and file lists never leave your device and are never sent to us or any third party.

Third-party services

The app integrates only with your device's own system features:

  • The Android "open with" / share system dialogs, when you choose to open or share a file with another app you select. What happens after that is governed by that other app's privacy policy.

The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If future versions add features that handle data (for example cloud storage or network connections), this policy will be updated before those features ship.
