English

Nemanli Driver ("the App") is a driver-side ride-hailing application. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data we collect

DataPurpose
Phone numberAccount creation, OTP sign-in, identification
Name, profile photoDriver profile shown to riders
Driver documents (license, ID, vehicle papers)Onboarding verification
Bank / card detailsPayouts of earnings
Device locationShow your position on the map, match nearby ride requests, navigate during a trip
Ride & earnings historyTrip records, payment history, statistics
Chat messagesRider–driver communication during a ride

Authentication tokens are stored on the device using the platform secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

2. How we use data

Provide the core service: receive, accept and complete ride requests; show your live location on the map and enable navigation; process earnings and payouts; enable rider–driver chat and ratings; improve reliability and prevent fraud. We do not sell personal data.

3. Location permission

The App requests location access (foreground) to display your position on the map and to match you with nearby ride requests. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Data retention & deletion

Account, ride and document data are retained while your account is active. You may request account closure and data deletion from within the App ("Close Account") or by emailing us.

5. Children

The App is intended for professional drivers and is not directed at children under 16.

Kirish tokenlari qurilmada xavfsiz saqlashda (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) saqlanadi.
