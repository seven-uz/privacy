O'zbekcha

Halal Skaner ilovasi foydalanuvchi maxfiyligini to'liq hurmat qiladi.

Ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi. Halal Skaner hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumotni yig'maydi, saqlamaydi va uchinchi tomonlarga uzatmaydi. Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish yoki akkaunt talab qilinmaydi.

Kamera ruxsati. Ilova shtrix-kodni skanerlash uchun kamera ruxsatidan foydalanadi. Kamera orqali olingan tasvir faqat qurilmangizda, real vaqtda shtrix-kodni aniqlash uchun ishlatiladi — hech qanday rasm yoki video saqlanmaydi yoki serverga yuborilmaydi.

Ma'lumot bazasi. E-kodlar va mahsulotlar bazasi ilova ichida (oflayn) saqlanadi. Internet ulanishi talab qilinmaydi.

Bolalar. Ilova har qanday yoshdagi foydalanuvchilar uchun xavfsiz, chunki hech qanday ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi.

English

The Halal Skaner app fully respects user privacy.

No data collection. Halal Skaner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any third party. No sign-up or account is required.

Camera permission. The app uses the camera permission to scan barcodes. The camera feed is used only on-device, in real time, to detect the barcode — no image or video is ever saved or sent to a server.

Product database. The E-code and product database is bundled inside the app (offline). No internet connection is required.

Children. The app is safe for users of all ages, as no data is collected.
