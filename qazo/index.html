Ilova / App: Qazo (uz.seven.qazo)

O'zbekcha

Qazo ilovasi foydalanuvchi maxfiyligini to'liq hurmat qiladi.

Ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi. Qazo hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumotni yig'maydi, saqlamaydi va uchinchi tomonlarga uzatmaydi. Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish yoki akkaunt talab qilinmaydi.

Ma'lumot qayerda saqlanadi. Kiritgan barcha ma'lumotlar (qazo hisoblari, profillar, sozlamalar, statistika) faqat qurilmangizning ichki xotirasida (AsyncStorage) saqlanadi. Bu ma'lumot serverlarga yuborilmaydi.

Joylashuv. Ilova joylashuv ruxsatidan faqat namoz vaqtlari va qibla yo'nalishini hisoblash uchun foydalanadi. Koordinatalar faqat qurilmangizda keshlanadi; internet orqali hech qayerga yuborilmaydi. Joylashuvsiz ham shaharni qo'lda tanlab foydalanish mumkin.

Internet. Ilova ishlashi uchun internet talab qilinmaydi (barcha hisob — namoz vaqtlari, hijriy sana, qibla — qurilmada, oflayn amalga oshiriladi).

Ruxsatlar. Bildirishnoma ruxsati faqat kunlik/namoz vaqti eslatmalarini ko'rsatish uchun ishlatiladi. Hech qanday ma'lumot yig'ish uchun emas.

Zaxira. Zaxira/tiklash funksiyasi ma'lumotni JSON ko'rinishida siz tanlagan joyga (clipboard yoki Share orqali) eksport qiladi; bu ma'lumot to'liq sizning nazoratingizda bo'ladi.

Bolalar. Ilova har qanday yoshdagi foydalanuvchilar uchun xavfsiz, chunki hech qanday ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi.

English

The Qazo app fully respects user privacy.

No data collection. Qazo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any third party. No sign-up or account is required.

Where data is stored. All data you enter (prayer counts, profiles, settings, statistics) is stored only in your device's internal storage (AsyncStorage). This data is never sent to any server.

Location. The app uses the location permission only to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. Coordinates are cached only on your device and never sent anywhere over the internet. You can also select your city manually without granting location access.

Internet. No internet connection is required for the app to work (all calculations — prayer times, Hijri date, qibla — are performed on-device, offline).

Permissions. The notification permission is used only to show daily and prayer-time reminders. It is never used to collect any data.

Backup. The backup/restore feature exports your data as JSON to a destination you choose (clipboard or Share); this data remains entirely under your control.

Children. The app is safe for users of all ages, as no data is collected.
